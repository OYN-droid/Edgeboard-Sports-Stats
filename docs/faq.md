# Frequently asked questions

## Is EdgeBoard showing live data?

Only when a league and domain are explicitly certified and labeled live. The default repository experience is sample mode. Fixture, cached, delayed, stale, partial, and offline fallback states remain visible.

## Is Research Quality a predicted win probability?

No. It summarizes evidence coverage, freshness, validation, and source agreement.

## Does EdgeBoard recommend bets or place wagers?

No. Edge Markets and the Parlay Builder are research tools. The Research Slip is not a placed wager.

## Where is my Workspace stored?

The default Workspace is stored locally in this browser. Clearing browser data can remove it. Export important work and review the storage disclosure before relying on it.

## Why is a result unavailable?

EdgeBoard suppresses results when identities are ambiguous, required fields are absent, data is too stale, a market is suspended, or the available evidence cannot support the requested claim. Refine the scope, try a related supported topic, or check Data Status.
