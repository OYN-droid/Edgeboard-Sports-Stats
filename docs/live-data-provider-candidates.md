## Excluded approaches

- No scraping of league sites, sportsbook pages, UFCStats, BoxRec, timing sites, or unofficial endpoints.
- No browser-direct vendor calls, even when a provider's examples put keys in query strings.
- No community package is treated as a data license or reliability commitment.
- Official league feeds may be used only where public/commercial API access and display/retention rights are explicit; an official website is not automatically an API.

## Proof-of-concept evaluation sheet

Require each candidate to provide: exact product/league/domain list; real or structurally faithful sample; stable IDs; status and correction lifecycle; historical start dates; book/market coverage; archive frequency; latency/SLA; rate and overage behavior; cache/retention/redistribution/derived-data/media terms; attribution; support escalation; sandbox/replay; and pricing separated by sport, domain, and environment.

Score the resulting contract and payload, not marketing breadth: identity 15%, completeness 15%, correctness/corrections 15%, latency/reliability 15%, rights/retention 15%, historical depth 10%, market depth 5%, integration effort 5%, and three-year cost 5%. A provider fails regardless of score if its rights do not cover EdgeBoard's public research use.
