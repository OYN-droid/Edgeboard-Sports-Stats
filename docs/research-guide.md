# Research guide

Edge Intelligence exposes a research plan before presenting a supported answer. A Research Session retains the question, evidence, statistics, visualizations, comparisons, insights, counterarguments, markets, Research Quality, notes, and follow-up questions.

Prefer explicit questions: name the entity, statistic, date or last-N window, competition, split, and comparison scope. If an explicit query scope conflicts with navigation, EdgeBoard shows the interpreted scope instead of silently mixing contexts.

Use **Stats** for observed evidence, **Betting** for provider-confirmed markets, and **Both** when compatible current market metadata can be attached to factual research. Both mode never turns a historical statistic into a market line.

Open supporting data before sharing a conclusion. Check sample size, source, freshness, completion status, and counterarguments. Ambiguous identities, missing statistics, stale markets, and insufficient evidence should produce an unavailable or limited result—not a fabricated answer.
