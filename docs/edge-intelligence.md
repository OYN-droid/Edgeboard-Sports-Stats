# Edge Intelligence guide

Edge Intelligence is the structured research workflow—not a free-form answer generator. It first classifies the question, resolves canonical identities and scope, builds a visible plan, and collects supported evidence. The final summary, counterarguments, Research Quality, and follow-up questions are derived from that structured result.

Good questions name the subject, statistic or market, period, split, and comparison. For example: “Compare these two WNBA players’ assists over their last 10 completed games using the same filters.” Unsupported stats, ambiguous names, incomplete events, and incompatible markets remain unresolved rather than being guessed.

Use a **Research Session** to preserve the original question, plan, evidence, visuals, comparisons, insights, counterarguments, markets, quality state, notes, and follow-ups. Refresh creates a new revision; it does not overwrite the saved snapshot. See [Research sessions](research-sessions.md) and [Research guide](research-guide.md).
